================================================================================
                    MONTHLY RETURN PLAN (MRP) - SCHEME LOGIC
================================================================================

1. SCHEME OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scheme Name            : Monthly Return Plan (MRP)
Scheme Classification  : Non-Cumulative Fixed Term Deposit / Monthly Income Scheme (MIS)
Deposit Nature         : One-time Lump-Sum Contribution (Upfront Investment)
Tenure Duration        : 1 Year to 6 Years (12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72 Months)
Payout Frequency       : Monthly Interest Payout to Customer / Member
Maturity Settlement    : 100% of Initial Contribution (Principal) Returned at Maturity


2. CORE FINANCIAL LOGIC & FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The customer makes an upfront contribution (e.g., Rs. 1,00,000).
- Interest is calculated on the principal based on the annual interest rate.
- Rather than compounding to maturity, the interest is disbursed EVERY MONTH.
- At tenure completion (maturity date), the original principal amount is refunded.

Formulas:
  a) Annual Interest = (Contribution Amount * Annual Interest Rate %) / 100
  b) Monthly Return  = Annual Interest / 12
                     = (Contribution Amount * Annual Interest Rate %) / 1200
  c) Total Return    = (Monthly Return * Tenure in Months) + Principal


3. INTEREST RATE SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tenure (Years) | General Category (%) | Senior Citizen Category (%) | Senior Citizen Extra
---------------+----------------------+------------------------------+---------------------
    Year 1     |        10.00%        |            10.50%            |       +0.50%
    Year 2     |        10.50%        |            11.00%            |       +0.50%
    Year 3     |        11.00%        |            11.50%            |       +0.50%
    Year 4     |        11.50%        |            12.00%            |       +0.50%
    Year 5     |        12.00%        |            12.50%            |       +0.50%
    Year 6     |        12.50%        |            13.00%            |       +0.50%

* Rate Rule:
  - Base interest rate starts at 10.0% for Year 1 and increases by 0.5% each year up to 12.5%.
  - Senior Citizens receive an additional +0.50% incentive across all years (10.5% to 13.0%).

6. SYSTEM IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATIONS (Multicred System)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Entity: SchemeType
  - code                     : MIS / MRP
  - name                     : Monthly Return Plan (MRP)
  - calculation_type         : LUMP_SUM
  - default_frequency        : MONTHLY

Entity: DepositScheme
  - scheme_type              : MIS
  - collection_frequency     : ONE_TIME
  - interest_payout_frequency: MONTHLY
  - senior_citizen_extra_rate: 0.50

Entity: DepositSchemeInterestRate (Tiers)
  - Tier 1: Tenure = 1, Tenure Unit = YEARS, Interest Rate = 10.00%, Senior Extra = 0.50%
  - Tier 2: Tenure = 2, Tenure Unit = YEARS, Interest Rate = 10.50%, Senior Extra = 0.50%
  - Tier 3: Tenure = 3, Tenure Unit = YEARS, Interest Rate = 11.00%, Senior Extra = 0.50%
  - Tier 4: Tenure = 4, Tenure Unit = YEARS, Interest Rate = 11.50%, Senior Extra = 0.50%
  - Tier 5: Tenure = 5, Tenure Unit = YEARS, Interest Rate = 12.00%, Senior Extra = 0.50%
  - Tier 6: Tenure = 6, Tenure Unit = YEARS, Interest Rate = 12.50%, Senior Extra = 0.50%

Maturity Calculation Logic:
  - Monthly Payout  = round((Deposit Amount * Rate / 100) / 12, 2)
  - Maturity Amount = Deposit Amount (Principal returned in full at maturity)
